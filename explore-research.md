# Explore / The Resort — Real Content Research (Oct 16, 2026)

Sources: official site https://www.ezelleapts.com (+ /amenities/, /gallery/),
October 2026 Ezelle newsletter (email, Oct 1), follow-up resident emails.

## Amenities (guest-friendly)

### Resort-style pool
Picturesque pool with tranquil lounging area, hammocks, cabanas and sundeck.
Open **dawn to dusk**. Up to **2 guests per leaseholder**, and the leaseholder
must be present with guests at all times. **No glass** anywhere near the pool —
if glass breaks, Osceola County requires the pool to be fully drained and closed.
No parties, loud music, or after-hours swimming. There's a TV by the fireplace;
the remote can be checked out at the leasing office with a driver's license
($50 replacement charge if not returned).

### Clubhouse / Clubroom
Stylish clubroom with kitchen and fireplace for cozy gatherings. Has a
**coffee station** and a **wine tap** featuring rotating local winery flavors
(October 2026: Island Grove Winery berry sangria). This is where resident
events happen (wine nights, holiday parties). Food trucks regularly park right
in front of the clubhouse.

### Fitness center
Ultramodern fitness center (24/7 access typical for Bozzuto; confirm with office).

### Yoga studio + stretch room
Serene dedicated yoga/stretch room, separate from the main gym.

### Co-work space
Contemporary coworking space in the clubhouse, plus business center and
conference rooms.

### Fire pit & grills
Outdoor fire pit and grills for evening gatherings.

### Wetland nature trail
Lush wetland nature trail around the community with **outdoor exercise
equipment** along the way.

### Paw spa + dog park
Indulgent pet spa (wash station) and a dog park. The dog park is the **only**
off-leash area; dogs must be leashed everywhere else. Pet waste-bag stations
throughout the community. Pet policy: dogs and cats allowed, $500 fee,
$25/month pet rent, 2-pet limit.

### Car spa
On-site car wash area. Limit **1 hour per turn** so others get a chance.
Residents only, no commercial vehicles. Don't vacuum liquids (damages equipment).

### EV charging
EV charging stations on site (Blink app — ask the office for help setting it
up). Do **not** park in EV spots without an EV — subject to tow. EV-equipped
garages also available ($300/mo including electric + charger).

### Games & lounge
Ping-pong, bocce ball, cornhole, gaming room, and outdoor lounge areas.

### Terraced courtyard
Courtyard designed for stargazing in the evening.

## Good-to-know (community rules guests should see)

- **Smoke-free community** — no smoking anywhere: not in the apartment, on the
  patio/balcony, in breezeways, or at any outdoor amenity. Fines apply, and
  residents are responsible for their guests knowing this.
- **Gated community** with night patrol. Courtesy officer: Trooper David
  Morales — office (407) 499-1027, prompt 4, or text (407) 399-7335.
- **Quiet hours:** 10 PM – 7 AM (loud music/shouting never OK).
- **Valet trash** nightly except Friday and Saturday nights. Never leave trash
  on the ground outside the door (fines).
- **Packages:** Luxer One package lockers; the Luxer One app can issue a
  temporary access code if no pickup code arrives.
- **Gate upgrade in progress** (announced Oct 1, ~2½ weeks): possible brief
  delays entering/exiting.
- Leasing office: 900 Laura Oaks Blvd, (844) 977-4290. Hours: Mon/Tue/Thu
  9–6, Wed 11–6, Fri 9–5, Sat 10–5, Sun closed.
