Welcome screen — 5 concepts

Research shaped these: hotel tech research says the guest's name is the whole moment ("we've had the guest name on the screen forever" — SONIFI); onboarding research says one emotion, staggered entrance, no clutter. So each concept varies the mood + transition, not the content. Click any TV to preview its transition to the home screen. Sample guests: Jen & Kevin.

1 · Maison Classic

Warm, brand-consistent. Staggered rise-in (eyebrow → names → message → key hint), then a simple crossfade to home. Safest, matches the app's existing voice.

2 · Drift slowest

Ambient and calm. Background breathes on a 26-second Ken Burns loop; names arrive late and unhurried. Two-second cinematic fade to home. For guests who'll see it first thing in the morning.

3 · Iris most theatrical

Pure drama. Names on near-black, then a circular iris opens from the center to reveal the home screen — like a theater curtain. Unforgettable, but the boldest of the five.

4 · Split card

The homepage is already there, blurred behind a frosted welcome card — a mirror of how our windows open. On keypress the card lifts away and the home screen sharpens into focus. Feels native to the app's design language.

5 · Dawn time-aware

A gradient sky that slowly brightens on a loop — morning / afternoon / evening variants from the clock. "Good morning, Jen & Kevin" feels alive in a way static screens don't. Pairs naturally with the automation that turns the TV on.
