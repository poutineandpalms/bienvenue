Folio — who owes what (5 concepts)

Sample trip: 5 people, $1,450 total. Research note: Tricount's own balance screen color-codes members red/green by net and suggests the simplest settle-up; Splitwise leads with "who owes whom" rather than raw shares. Every concept below leads with the net, not the share — that's the number a guest actually looks for.

A · Chips row

What we have now, refined. Trip total + one compact chip per person + QR, all in one row. Glanceable for 2–3 people; wraps to two rows at five. Simplest, least new UI.

B · Settle-up

Skip per-person nets entirely — show the minimum payments that settle everything, like Tricount's balance tab does. Three lines and the group is done. Most actionable, but hides the "what did I spend" detail (still in the expense list).
