Design round 3 — Treatment 2 vs 3, across the week

Fair criticism on the last round: Treatment 3’s “themed gradient” was shy to the point of invisible. Here it is, unmistakable — a real wash of the theme colour over the photo, with the interface staying warm-neutral. Every destination below appears in both treatments: T2 re-keys the whole interface to the moment; T3 keeps ivory-on-charcoal and lets only the gradient change.

How to compare: T2 = colour in the tiles, chips and focus. T3 = same warmth everywhere; the only thing that changes is the gradient. The gold Maison identity survives in T3 as the wordmark and the focus tile.

Two honest observations from building these: T2’s magic fades by the fourth destination — the interface keeps introducing itself. T3’s risk is the opposite: it can look the same every day, and the day’s identity has to come from the photo and the headline (which, arguably, it should). The gradient in T3 is now loud enough to feel; whether it’s loud enough to feel different is your call.
