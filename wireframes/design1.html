Visual design — the home screen, three directions

Same layout you approved (Concept E refined, arrival day), same generated hero photo in all three — only the visual identity changes. This is the moment we stop arguing about boxes: pick the feeling, and the overlays inherit it. Wi-Fi is on the home row because it’s day one; from day two it nests inside Your stay.

How to judge: squint. Which one feels like walking into your place at golden hour? Type, colour, and tile treatment are the whole decision — proportions stay as wireframed.
Direction 1 — “Maison” · boutique hotel
Deep green and gold, serif display type (the Playfair already living in the app), frosted dark tiles. The most “resort” of the three — quiet, warm, a little formal.
