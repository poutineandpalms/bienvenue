Your Room — round 2: three kinds of control

Three ideas, one rule: lamps, ambience, and services each get their own visual language, because pressing a button that does something (vacuum, goodnight) is not the same as flipping a switch. Repetitive helper text is gone — a lamp chip shows an icon, a name, and a glow dot. Nothing more.

A — Calm strip one row per controller type
Lamps as minimal chips (icon · name · glow). Ambience as a single segmented pill — not tiles. Climate dial off to the side. Services in their own zone at the bottom, styled as actions, never as lamps.
